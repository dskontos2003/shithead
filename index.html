📖 How to play
Goal: Get rid of all your cards.
Play: Cards must be ≥ the top card of the pile.
No play: Take the whole pile.
Hand: Keep 3 cards until the deck runs out.
Table: 3 stacked slots per player (face-up on face-down). Play when hand is empty.
🔢 2 – Restart: any card OK next.
🔢 5 – Reverse: next player must play ≤5.
🔢 10 – Burns the pile. Play again.
Four of a kind on top burns the pile. Same player goes again.
Face-down played blind — if invalid, take the pile.
